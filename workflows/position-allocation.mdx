# Position Allocation Management Workflow

This workflow explains how admins define **which recruiter owns which job roles** inside the RMS.

## Add Position Allocation

1. Click **Add Allocation**
2. Select:
   - **Category**
   - **Sub-Category**
   - **Recruiter**
3. Click **Save**

The selected recruiter is now responsible for all jobs under those roles.

![Image](/images/image-1.png)

## View Allocation

1. Click **View**
2. See allocation details:
   - Category & sub-category
   - Assigned recruiter
   - Status
   - Created and updated timestamps

![Image](/images/image-2.png)

## Edit Allocation

1. Click **Edit** next to an allocation
2. Update:
   - Recruiter
   - Category or sub-category
3. Save changes

Updates apply immediately across recruiter dashboards and job views.

![Image](/images/image-3.png)

## Delete Allocation

1. Click **Delete**
2. Confirm the action

Deletion behavior:

- Allocation is **soft-deleted**
- Recruiter immediately loses access to those job roles
- Jobs and candidates are no longer visible to that recruiter

![Image](/images/image-4.png)

## Recruiter Experience

1. Recruiter logs in
2. System fetches **My Allocations**
3. Recruiter can only access:
   - Jobs
   - Campaigns
   - Candidates

linked to their assigned categories and sub-categories.

## Search & Filter

Admins and managers can filter allocations by:

- Recruiter name
- Category
- Sub-category

Recruiters automatically see **only their own allocations**.

![Image](/images/image-5.png)

## Where It Is Used

Position Allocation directly controls:

- Recruiter job listing visibility
- Campaign access permissions
- Candidate tracker filtering
- Recruiter dashboards

## Summary

Position Allocation Management ensures **clear ownership of job roles** across recruiters.  
It prevents overlap, restricts access correctly, and helps scale recruitment teams with structured control and accountability.
