# Category Management Workflow

This workflow shows how administrators create and manage **Categories → Sub Categories**, and how DDIM-AI is progressively applied at each level to support JD creation, candidate evaluation, and profile articulation in the RMS.

## Workflow Overview

1. Admin opens Category Management
2. Creates or edits a Category
3. Adds Sub Categories (role clusters)
4. Defines DDIM knowledge and competencies
5. Reviews the complete hierarchy
6. Saves and uses it across RMS

## Step 1: Create Category

1. Click **New Category**
2. Enter:
   - Category Name
   - Description
3. Select **Status**
   - Active
   - Inactive

### DDIM-AI Integration

At the Category level, DDIM defines:

- Broad knowledge domains
- Applicable safety or technical standards
- Overall difficulty context (Basic → Intermediate → Expert)

This allows RMS to group roles correctly within the DDIM knowledge framework.

## Step 2: Add Sub Category

A Sub Category defines a **role cluster** and is the primary layer where DDIM intelligence is stored.

1. Inside a Category, click **Add Sub Category**
2. Enter:
   - Name
   - Description
   - Total Experience
3. Click **Save**

![Screenshot 2025 12 19 115124](/images/Screenshot2025-12-19115124.png)

### DDIM-AI Integration

Each Sub Category may include:

- Primary objective
- Core responsibilities
- Coverage areas
- Interfaces
- Competence requirements
- Certifications
- Key standards
- KPIs

### DDIM Knowledge Levels Supported

- Level 1: Fundamentals
- Level 2: Applied and Scenario Questions
- Level 3: Expert Diagnostic Questions
- Level 4: Case-Based and Incident Questions
- Level 5: Behavioral Competencies (C1–C15)

These levels power:

- JD auto-generation
- AI-based evaluation
- Interview preparation
- Competency scoring

![Screenshot 2025 12 19 115229](/images/Screenshot2025-12-19115229.png)

## Step 3: Edit Category Structure

Admins can update:

- Category name
- Description
- Status
- Sub Category details
- Sub Sub Category details
- DDIM knowledge levels
- Behavioral competency matrix

All updates are reflected immediately across:

- JD Creation
- Profile Articulation
- Campaign Question Banks
- AI Interviews
- Candidate Evaluation

## Step 4: View Category

Viewing a category displays:

- Complete hierarchy
- Sub Categories and Sub Sub Categories
- DDIM Knowledge Universe (Levels 1–5)
- Behavioral Competency Matrix (C1–C15)
- Standards, KPIs, responsibilities, competencies
- Target companies

This view acts as the **single source of truth** for role intelligence.

![Screenshot 2025 12 19 115426](/images/Screenshot2025-12-19115426.png)

## Step 5: Delete Category

1. Click **Delete**
2. Confirm deletion

The Category and all nested DDIM metadata are removed from the RMS.

![Screenshot 2025 12 19 115502](/images/Screenshot2025-12-19115502.png)

## DDIM-AI Integration Summary

DDIM is embedded directly into the Category workflow.

As admins move from:

- Category → Sub Category

DDIM progressively adds:

- Structured knowledge (Levels 1–4)
- Behavioral competencies (Level 5)
- Standards, KPIs, and certifications
- Role objectives and responsibilities

This ensures that JD creation, candidate evaluation, and profile articulation remain accurate, standardized, and AI-ready.

## Summary

Category Management is the foundation of RMS intelligence.

Hierarchy:

- **Category** → Role domain
- **Sub Category** → Role cluster enriched with DDIM knowledge

This structured flow enables:

- Accurate JD creation
- Standardized interviews
- Consistent candidate evaluation
- AI-driven role intelligence across the RMS
